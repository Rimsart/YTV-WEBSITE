A Movement Born From Youth Ambition

Y-TV (Youth Transformation for Vision 2060) was founded on the belief that Kenya's greatest asset is its dynamic, creative, and energetic youth population. We are the flagship catalyst uniting young Kenyans across all 47 counties.

Our Vision

A transformed Kenya by 2060 - driven by empowered, innovative, and patriotic youth.

Our Mission

To empower young people to realize their potential and actively contribute to Kenya's transformation by 2060.

Our Objectives

  • Shift youth from passive onlookers to active drivers of national policy.
  • Bring together diverse talents from grassroots innovators to tech pioneers.
  • Directly support Kenya's long-term developmental roadmap (Vision 2060).
